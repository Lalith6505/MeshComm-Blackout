Privacy Policy

MeshComm Blackout guarantees complete cryptographic privacy and zero centralized data retention.

1. Complete Offline Architecture

MeshComm Blackout does not communicate with, register with, or authenticate against any central servers or cloud platforms. Communication occurs strictly over direct device-to-device radio links (Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi-Fi Aware NAN).

2. End-to-End Encryption

All direct messages, audio bursts, and private coordinates are encrypted on-device prior to transmission using Curve25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305. Relay nodes pass encrypted ciphertexts without capability to inspect content or identities.

3. Zero Analytics & Zero Logs

No user identifiers, telemetry packets, or usage logs are collected or uploaded. The application contains zero advertising frameworks and zero third-party software development kits (SDKs).

4. StrongBox Key Isolation

Private cryptographic identity keys are generated and stored within the sandboxed Android Keystore hardware security module (StrongBox where available). Keys never leave physical hardware.

5. Contact Information

For questions or verification regarding MeshComm Blackout, please refer to the project repository documentation.
